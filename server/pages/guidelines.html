Siegel community guidelines

Last updated 19 October 2026. These are the rules behind our terms, in plain words. The headings are the same as the choices you get when you report something.

Sexual content involving a minor

Never. That means pictures, videos or text of a sexual nature involving anyone under 18, asking a child for such content, or talking to a child to win their trust for sex ("grooming"). We ban the account and report it to the authorities. If someone does this to you, report it and tell an adult you trust. You've done nothing wrong.

Other sexual content without consent

Don't share intimate images of someone without their consent, and don't threaten to ("sextortion"). Don't send sexual content to people who didn't ask for it.

Harassment or threats

No bullying, stalking, threats of violence, or pushing someone to hurt themselves. If someone keeps writing after you've said no, block them.

Hate

Don't attack people for their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, sex, gender identity or sexual orientation.

Violence or terrorism

Don't plan, promote or celebrate violence or terrorism.

Spam or scams

No mass messages to people who don't know you, fake offers, phishing, or attempts to get money, passwords or codes. Siegel will never ask you for your code or PIN.

Impersonation

Don't pretend to be another person, a company, a public official or Siegel's staff. Usernames that look like those are reserved.

Something else

Anything else illegal, malware, or attacks on the service.

Keeping yourself safe

  • People you don't know arrive as message requests. Nothing they send reaches your chats until you accept, and they don't learn whether you read it.
  • Block anyone, any time. They aren't told.
  • Share your contact link or QR code only with people you trust: it lets them chat with you straight away.

When you report

Only the messages you choose are sent to us, readable. The person you report isn't told who reported them. Reports about children are reviewed within 24 hours, others within 7 days. If we act, the account is told why and can appeal.
