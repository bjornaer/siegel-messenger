Who we are

Who may use Siegel

You must be at least 14 in Spain, and 16 or your country's age of digital consent elsewhere.

Siegel asks your birth month and year when you sign up. They stay on your phone; we only learn whether you're under 18. Accounts of people under 18 get extra protection: they can't be found by searching their @username, only by people they gave their contact link to (or who have their number, if they turn that on), and only their contacts can add them to groups.

Your account

  • One account per phone number. You must be able to receive SMS at that number.
  • Your messages are stored only on your phone. We can't restore them if you lose it.
  • Keep your phone secure. A Registration Lock PIN stops someone who takes your number from taking your account.
  • Accounts with no device connected for 180 days are deleted.

What isn't allowed

Our community guidelines explain each of these in plain words. In short, don't use Siegel to:

  • share, request or produce sexual content involving anyone under 18, or contact a child for sexual purposes. We ban the account and report it to the authorities;
  • harass, threaten or stalk anyone, or share intimate images of someone without their consent;
  • attack people for who they are (hate), or promote violence or terrorism;
  • send spam or run scams;
  • pretend to be someone else, including public officials or Siegel's staff;
  • break the law, spread malware, or attack or overload the service.

What we can see, and what we do

Messages are end-to-end encrypted: we can't read them, and we don't scan them, on our servers or on your phone. We act on what people report to us. A report contains the messages the reporter chose to send us, readable. We may also act on your @username, invite links and group names, which others can see.

If you break these terms we may remove your @username or an invite link, suspend your account for a time, or ban it. A banned number can't register again. Each time, we tell you in the app what we did, why, and how to appeal. Appeal by writing to {{template "contact" .}}; a person reviews every appeal.

Reporting

In the app, long-press a message and choose Report, or use a chat's or group's menu. A message request also offers Report and block. You can also write to {{template "contact" .}}. Reports about children are reviewed within 24 hours, other reports within 7 days.

Authorities

Our point of contact for authorities and for users is {{template "contact" .}}, in English or Spanish. We answer valid legal requests. Because of how Siegel is built we can only provide what our privacy policy lists: never message content, contacts or chat history.
