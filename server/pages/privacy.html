Siegel Messenger: Privacy policy

Draft under legal review. Last updated 15 October 2026. It describes what the app and server do today; we update it whenever that changes.

Who we are

Siegel Messenger ("Siegel", "we") is operated by {{if .Operator}}{{.Operator}}{{else}}its developer, whose legal name and address will be published here before public launch{{end}}. For privacy questions write to {{template "contact" .}}.

The short version

Your messages, your profile name and your contacts' names are end-to-end encrypted. We cannot read them, and they are stored only on your phone. We hold the minimum needed to deliver messages: a keyed hash of your phone number, your @username, public encryption keys, and messages waiting for delivery, which we can't read either.

We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and we don't sell or rent data. Server logs never contain phone numbers, message data or IP addresses, and are deleted after 7 days.

What stays on your phone

Your message history, contacts, your contacts' names and your settings are stored only on your device, in an encrypted database. We have no copy and no backup of them. If you lose your phone, reset it or delete the app, that history is gone.

Who else processes data

  • Hosting: Hetzner Online GmbH, Germany, servers in the EU.
  • SMS delivery: GatewayAPI (OnlineCity ApS), Denmark. Receives your phone number and code to send the SMS.
  • Google: Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers the content-free wake-up notifications, and the Play Integrity API confirms that a registration comes from a genuine copy of the app before we send an SMS. Google sees that these happen, never your messages.

These companies process data only on our behalf, under data processing agreements.

Your rights

You can access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, and receive it in a portable form. Delete your account in the app under Settings → Delete account, or see how to delete it without the app. Deletion removes your server data at once, and it leaves our backups within 24 hours. For anything else write to {{template "contact" .}}. You can also complain to the Spanish data protection authority (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, www.aepd.es).

Children

You must be at least 14 in Spain, and 16 or your country's age of digital consent elsewhere, to use Siegel.

Security

Messages use the MLS protocol (RFC 9420) and are encrypted end to end. Connections use TLS 1.3 with certificate pinning. The keys that protect phone-number hashes are held in a separate key store, not in the database. Our source code is public at github.com/bjornaer/siegel-messenger.

Changes

We announce material changes in the app before they take effect.
