Delete your Siegel account

In the app

Open Siegel, go to Settings → Delete account and confirm. Your account is deleted from our servers straight away, and from our backups within 24 hours. Your messages and chats are stored only on your phone and are erased with it.

Without the app

Write to {{template "contact" .}} from any address and tell us the phone number of the account. To protect you from someone else deleting your account, we send a confirmation code to that number before we delete anything. We delete the account within 30 days of your request.

If you simply uninstall Siegel, your account is also deleted automatically after 180 days in which none of your devices connect.

What is deleted

  • The keyed hash of your phone number (we never store the number itself)
  • Your @username, encrypted profile, public encryption keys and devices
  • Any messages still waiting for delivery, push tokens and your Registration Lock

What is kept

Your former @username stays reserved for 30 days, without any link to you, so nobody can impersonate you right away. Nothing else is kept.
